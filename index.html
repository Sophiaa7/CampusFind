Safety Guidelines

  • Never publicly share sensitive information (IMEI, full serial numbers, passwords).
  • Always verify ownership before handing over an item.
  • Prefer meeting in public campus locations or official recovery points.
  • Report suspicious activity to campus security.
  • CampusFind does not store or display private verification details publicly.
